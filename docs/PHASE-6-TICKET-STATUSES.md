# Support Ticket Status Lifecycle

```text
open
  → awaiting_staff
  → awaiting_chapter
  → under_review
  → escalated
  → resolved
  → closed
```

- **Open:** Newly created ticket.
- **Awaiting Staff:** The chapter has sent the latest reply.
- **Awaiting Chapter:** Prayer Project staff sent the latest reply.
- **Under Review:** Staff are investigating or consulting another department.
- **Escalated:** The issue requires elevated attention.
- **Resolved:** Staff believe the issue has been addressed.
- **Closed:** The conversation is read-only for chapter users.

Authorized staff may move between statuses as operationally necessary. Chapter users may close an accessible active ticket but cannot assign, escalate, or mark it resolved.
